This show's subtitle font and drawings, for the checks above:

- **This font draws the particle 了 like the digit 7.**
  - 了 is a grammatical particle: right after a verb or adjective (走了, 来了, 太多了, 亏了多少) or closing the sentence.
  - 7 is a number: next to other digits (17, 70, 7.5), before a measure word or unit (7斤, 7个, 7天, 7万, 7米), or after 第 (第7).
  - Replace 7 with 了 only when the particle reading fits the grammar and the number reading does not: the 7 itself becomes 了 in `zh_fix` (太多7 → 太多了, 你说7算 → 你说了算). Never delete the 7, never keep it next to an added 了.
